Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a ride?

Tap the + button on the home screen. Fill in the title, start/end locations, date, and ride details. Your ride will be visible to nearby riders.

How do I join a ride?

Browse rides on the home screen, tap on one to see details, then press "Join Ride". You'll receive notifications about any updates.

How do I navigate to a ride's meeting point?

Open the ride details and tap the Google Maps or Waze button next to the start location. The app will open your preferred navigation app with directions.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Open the ride details and tap the flag icon in the top-right corner. Select a report reason. From the same panel you can block the ride organizer so their rides no longer appear in your feed. Vojo support reviews reports and may hide content or restrict accounts.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile, scroll to "Danger Zone", and tap "Delete Account". You can also use our web deletion instructions. Export anything you want to keep first. Deleting an account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription.

How do I turn off notifications?

Go to Profile and toggle the push notifications switch off. You can also manage notifications in your device's system settings.

What bike types are supported?

Vojo supports all bike categories: Sport, Cruiser, Adventure, Enduro, Touring, and Scooter. You can filter rides by bike type when browsing.

Is Vojo free?

Creating rides, joining rides, and core garage record-keeping are free. A limited AI quota may be available. Optional Vojo Pro products unlock additional AI-powered garage features. Available products and current prices are shown by your store. See our Subscription Policy.

What data is sent to AI?

Only after you agree, the text, motorcycle or maintenance details, and selected photos needed for your request are sent through Vojo to the AI provider identified in our Privacy Policy. You can decline and continue using non-AI features.
