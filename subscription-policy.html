Subscription Policy

Effective and last updated: July 14, 2026

1. Free and Pro features

Creating, finding, and joining rides and core garage record-keeping do not require a Pro purchase. Vojo may provide a limited free quota for selected AI functions. Vojo Pro unlocks the AI feature access described in the purchase screen, which may include workshop chat and diagnostics, repair guides, part suggestions, component profiles, odometer-photo recognition, and service schedules.

2. Products and price

Depending on your store and region, Vojo may offer monthly or annual auto-renewing subscriptions and a non-consumable lifetime purchase. Availability is not guaranteed. The product name, local price, currency, taxes, billing period, introductory offer, and renewal terms shown in the Apple App Store or Google Play purchase sheet immediately before confirmation are the terms of your purchase. Vojo does not directly process or store full payment-card details.

3. Trials and introductory offers

A free trial or other introductory offer applies only when the store shows it and confirms your eligibility. The store purchase sheet controls its duration and price. Unless you cancel before the deadline shown by the store, a trial for an auto-renewing product converts to a paid subscription at the displayed renewal price.

4. Renewal and cancellation

  • Auto-renewing subscriptions continue for the displayed billing period until cancelled. The store account is charged according to the timing disclosed in its purchase sheet and subscription settings.
  • iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Vojo → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Vojo → Cancel.
  • Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the paid period. You keep Pro access until the entitlement expires unless the store states otherwise.

Deleting the App or your Vojo account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel in the store before deleting your account if you do not want future charges.

5. Lifetime purchase

Where offered, a lifetime purchase grants the covered Vojo Pro entitlement for the supported lifetime of the Vojo service, subject to these Terms. It is not an auto-renewing subscription. It does not guarantee that every future product, paid external service, or materially different feature will be included forever.

6. Restore, access, and changes

Use Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple or Google account to recover an eligible entitlement. Store propagation and network availability may delay access. We may change the free quota or the set of Pro features, but will not remove access already required by mandatory store or consumer rules. Store-managed price increases and any required consent or advance notice follow Apple or Google rules.

7. Refunds

Apple or Google processes the purchase and decides refund requests under its policy. For iOS, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For Android, use Google Play order history or support. Contact us if you need information to identify a Vojo transaction.

8. AI safety

AI OUTPUT MAY BE WRONG OR UNSAFE AND IS INFORMATIONAL ONLY. IT DOES NOT REPLACE A QUALIFIED MECHANIC. DO NOT RELY ON IT FOR SAFETY-CRITICAL WORK. HAVE BRAKES, TIRES, STEERING, SUSPENSION, FUEL, ELECTRICAL, STRUCTURAL, AND UNCERTAIN ISSUES INSPECTED BY A PROFESSIONAL.
